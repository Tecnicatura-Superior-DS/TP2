Who we are

NovaSpark Studio was founded in 2018 with a simple belief: great digital products come from a blend of creativity, strategy, and technical excellence. What started as a two-person team has grown into a full-service digital agency working with startups and enterprises alike.

Our team of designers, engineers, and strategists collaborates closely with clients to transform complex challenges into intuitive, high-performing digital solutions. We don't just build products — we build lasting partnerships.
